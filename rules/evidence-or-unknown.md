# Evidence or unknown

Applies to brainstorm, vision, validation, competitor, persona and market work.

- Every factual claim (pain exists, market size, competitor pricing, user counts) carries an evidence item: `{ url, quote, retrievedAt }`, quoting the source.
- If you cannot find a source, the answer is `unknown` — not a guess, not "likely yes".
- Market sizes are ranges with their assumptions stated, never a single confident number.
- Synthetic personas or interviews are allowed only with `synthetic: true`.
- Prefer official APIs and respect site terms; never fabricate a URL or quote.
