# Rubric — PRD

Binary checks. Pass = at least 9 of 10 "yes".

1. Goals tie back to the vision's success metrics?
2. Non-goals include the vision's anti-goals?
3. Every story is one user-visible capability with a clear outcome ("so that…")?
4. Every story except `wont` has Given/When/Then acceptance criteria?
5. Acceptance criteria cover empty, error and edge states, not only the happy path?
6. The MVP cut is a walking skeleton through the whole core journey?
7. Nothing in the MVP lacks support in the validation evidence?
8. Every metric has a definition an engineer could implement?
9. Open questions are listed instead of guessed?
10. Scope cuts are recorded in the ledger with reasons?
