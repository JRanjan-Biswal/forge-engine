# Rubric — competitor teardown

Binary checks. Pass = at least 7 of 8 "yes".

1. 5–10 competitors, covering direct, indirect and substitute segments?
2. Every pricing, traction or user-count claim is sourced or omitted (no estimates)?
3. Each competitor has at least one real complaint saved through the evidence store?
4. Strengths explain why users choose the competitor, not marketing copy?
5. Weaknesses are evidenced (complaints, stale repos, missing features observed)?
6. Gap opportunities appear at 2 or more competitors, or are labelled as single-source?
7. No competitor that dominates search results for the category is missing?
8. The white space leads to a specific "only we" claim for the scorecard (C-08)?
