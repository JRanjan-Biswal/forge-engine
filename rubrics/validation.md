# Rubric — validation scorecard

Binary checks. Pass = at least 9 of 10 "yes". Use a different model than the producer as judge when available.

1. Every `yes` cites at least one saved evidence item that is directly about this problem and audience?
2. No criterion is answered from intuition alone (unsupported answers are `unknown`)?
3. C-01 evidence comes from at least 5 independent people or sources, not one thread?
4. Market size (C-04), if `yes`, is a sourced range with stated assumptions?
5. At least 5 competitors are analysed, including at least one substitute?
6. Review complaints are real quotes saved through the evidence store?
7. `risks[]` contains at least 3 concrete, specific risks with severities?
8. The verdict follows the computed score, or any deviation is explained in the ledger?
9. For `needs-experiment` / `pivot`, the cheapest next experiment is named with the signal that would change the verdict?
10. The decision memo would let a busy founder decide in under 2 minutes?
