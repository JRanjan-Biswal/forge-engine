# Rubric — product vision

Binary checks. Pass = at least 7 of 8 "yes".

1. The audience is a narrow first segment someone could actually list, not "everyone"?
2. The problem is written in the user's words and says how often it hurts?
3. The north star measures user value (not revenue, not signups)?
4. At least 3 anti-goals, each excluding something genuinely tempting?
5. Principles would settle a real future trade-off?
6. Success metrics are measurable and have targets?
7. No unsourced market or competitor claims (those belong to validation)?
8. The narrative makes the product understandable in under a minute?
